## Email us

Email is the fastest way to reach us, and it is the address we answer legal
notices at:

Email <a href={`mailto:${envConfigs.support_email}`}>{envConfigs.support_email}</a> — we reply to most messages within two business days.

## What to include

To help us answer on the first reply, please include:

- The email address on your account.
- Your order or subscription number, if your question is about a payment.
- What happened, and what you expected to happen.
- Screenshots, if the problem is something you can see.

## Things you can do yourself

Some requests are faster without waiting for us:

- **Cancel a subscription** — sign in, go to Settings, then Billing, and press
  Cancel next to your subscription. It takes effect at the end of the period
  you have already paid for.
- **Invoices and receipts** — Settings, then Billing.
- **Your credit balance and history** — Settings, then Credits.
- **Refunds** — see the [Refund & Cancellation Policy](/refund-policy).

## Privacy and data requests

Write to the same address to access, correct or export your personal data.
**To delete your account you do not need to write to us** — do it yourself from
Settings, then Profile, then Delete my account. Either way, please put "Privacy
request" in the subject line so it reaches the right person. See the
[Privacy Policy](/privacy-policy) for what we hold and how long we keep it.

## Business and legal enquiries

Partnerships, press, and anything that needs a signed agreement go to the same
address — please put "Business" or "Legal" in the subject line.
