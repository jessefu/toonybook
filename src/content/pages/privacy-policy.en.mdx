## 3. What we collect

**Account information.** Your name, email address, and password (stored only as
a one-way hash), plus your preferred language and any profile details you add.

**The material you give the Service to make a book.** The story idea you write,
the child's first name if you provide one, the page text after the story is
written, any edits you make to it, and the photo descriptions you write.

**Photos you upload.** Only if you choose to add them, and only to draw the
illustrations. Section 6 explains exactly what happens to them.

**The books you make.** The illustrations and story text we generate for you,
and the printable PDF, which we keep so you can read and re-download your book.

**Payment and billing information.** Which plan or pack you bought, the amount,
the currency, the status of the subscription, and the identifiers the payment
provider gives us. **We never receive or store your full card number** — it goes
directly to the payment provider.

**Technical and security information.** Your IP address, browser user-agent
string, and the times you signed in, which we record with your session so we can
keep your account secure and detect abuse. Our hosting provider also keeps
standard request logs for a limited period for security and debugging.

**Usage information.** Which features you use and when, how many credits you
spend, and whether a generation succeeded or failed — enough to operate,
support, and improve the Service.

**Photos are deleted when the book is done.** See section 6.

## 6. The photos you upload

This is how photo uploads work, end to end:

1. You upload a photo, and optionally say who is in it. It is stored in our own
   file storage — not on a third-party image host.
2. When you confirm a book, we make a private copy for that book, and that copy
   is what gets sent to the AI image provider so it can draw the illustrations.
3. **As soon as the book finishes, we delete both that copy and the original you
   uploaded.** The exception is a generation that fails: you may want to retry
   without uploading the photos again, so we keep them until you retry
   successfully or delete that book. (Abandon a draft before confirming and its
   copy is deleted too.)
4. The illustrations themselves, which are the finished artwork, stay in your
   book so you can read and re-download it. Delete the book and they are deleted
   with it.

One caveat, stated plainly: an AI provider receives the copy while the book is
being generated, and we cannot reach into their systems to delete it — their
retention is governed by their own policies, listed in section 7.

We use the photos for one thing only: drawing the illustrations you asked for.
They are never used to train models, our own or anyone else's. Deleting your
account removes everything that is left.

Please only upload photos you have the right to use. If a child appears in a
photo, you must be their parent or legal guardian, or have that person's
permission.

## 7. AI processing, and the service providers we use

Making a book means sending your material to third parties who do the work. We
share the minimum needed for each job:

- **AI generation.** Your story idea, the page text, and (if uploaded) the
  character photos are sent to the AI providers that write the text and draw the
  illustrations, so they can produce your book. This is the whole point of the
  Service, and it cannot be switched off while you are using it. We choose
  providers that contractually restrict their use of the material they process,
  and we do not permit them to use your content to train their own models.
- **Payments.** Checkout and subscription billing are handled by a payment
  provider, which receives your email address, the plan or pack you bought, and
  the amount. It handles your card details directly; we never see them.
- **Email.** Transactional email — sign-in links, receipts, notices — goes through
  an email delivery provider.
- **File storage.** Your books, illustrations, and the temporary photo copies are
  stored with a cloud object-storage provider.
- **Hosting.** The Service runs on a cloud hosting provider, which processes
  requests on our behalf.

The specific providers are configured per deployment, and their names are
available on request — email us and we will tell you exactly who processes your
data. We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it for
cross-context behavioural advertising.

We may also disclose information when the law requires it, when it is necessary
to protect someone's safety, or to establish or defend a legal claim. If the
Service is ever sold or merged, your information may transfer to the new owner
under the same commitments, and we will tell you before that happens.

## 8. Cookies and similar technologies

We use a small number of cookies and local-storage entries, and no advertising
ones:

- **Sign-in.** A session cookie that keeps you signed in. Without it, you cannot
  use your account.
- **Language.** A cookie remembering the language you chose, so the site does not
  reset on every visit.
- **Theme.** A local-storage entry remembering light or dark mode.

If analytics has been enabled for this deployment, it may set cookies to measure
traffic in aggregate. Analytics is used only to understand how the Service is
used; it is never used to identify you or to build advertising profiles. You can
block or delete cookies in your browser settings — the sign-in cookie is the
only one the Service cannot work without.

## 9. How long we keep it

- **Character photos:** the per-book copy is deleted as soon as the book
  finishes, and so is the original you uploaded. A generation that fails keeps
  both until you retry or delete that book.
- **Your books, illustrations and PDFs:** kept while your account is open, so you
  can always re-download them. Delete a book and it goes, except where we must
  keep a record of the transaction.
- **Account information:** kept while your account is open. Deleting your account
  from Settings then Profile removes your name, avatar, email address, sign-in
  credentials and everything else that identifies you, and we keep only what tax
  and accounting rules require — the billing records, for the period those rules
  set. Your email address is freed, so you can sign up again with it.
- **Security and request logs:** kept for a limited period by our hosting
  provider, for security and debugging.
