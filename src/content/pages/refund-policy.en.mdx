## The rule in one line

**Credits you have not spent are refundable within 14 days of purchase. Credits
you have already spent are not** — the illustration they paid for has been
generated and the computing behind it cannot be recovered.

## Credit packs (one-time purchases)

- **Not used at all:** email us within 14 days of the purchase and we refund it
  in full. No reason needed.
- **Partly used:** the pack is not refundable as a whole, because the credits you
  spent have already been consumed. Write to us anyway if something went wrong —
  we look at partial refunds case by case, and a technical problem on our side is
  always refunded.
- **Never expires:** credits from a pack stay in your account until you use them,
  so there is no deadline forcing you to spend them or lose them.

## Subscriptions

**How to cancel.** You can cancel yourself, at any time, in a few clicks — no
email, no phone call, no "contact us to cancel":

1. Sign in to your account.
2. Go to **Settings**, then **Billing**.
3. Find your subscription and press **Cancel**.
4. Confirm. That is the whole process.

**What cancelling does.** Cancellation takes effect at the end of the period you
have already paid for. Until then you keep full access, and you keep the credits
granted for that period. After that date, no further payments are taken. **We do
not charge a cancellation fee, and we do not require a notice period.**

**Refunds on subscriptions:**

- **Within 14 days of a charge, with none of that period's credits spent:** we
  refund that charge in full.
- **After that, or once credits from the period have been spent:** the current
  period is not refundable. Cancel instead, and the subscription simply stops
  renewing.
- **Accidental renewal?** If a renewal caught you by surprise and you have not
  spent any of the new period's credits, write to us — we refund renewals in that
  situation.

## What is never refundable

- **Credits already spent on a generated book.** Each page of illustration is a
  paid call to a third-party AI provider. Once it has run, the cost is gone and
  cannot be recovered, which is why the credits for it cannot be returned.
- **A book that finished successfully** and that you then decided you did not
  want. Please read the free draft and check the story before you confirm — the
  illustrations are only drawn, and credits only spent, after you confirm.
- **Purchases past the windows above.**

## When credits come back automatically

**If a book fails to generate because of a problem on our side, the credits for
it are returned to your account automatically.** You do not need to ask, and you
do not need to email us. This covers provider outages, timeouts, and anything
else that stops a book from finishing. You will see the returned credits in
Settings, then Credits.

If you believe credits were spent on a generation that did not deliver, email us
— we will look at the specific book and put it right.

## Not happy with a book that did finish?

We cannot refund the credits, but we want to fix the problem. Tell us what went
wrong — an illustration that came out strange, text that missed the mark — and we
will look at what we can do, including re-running the generation where that is
possible. Reports like this are also how we improve the models we use.

## How to request a refund

Email <a href={`mailto:${envConfigs.support_email}`}>{envConfigs.support_email}</a>
with:

- the email address on your account;
- your order or subscription number, if you have it;
- what you would like refunded, and why (one line is enough).

**What happens next:**

- We reply within **two business days**.
- Approved refunds go back to the **original payment method**. We cannot refund
  to a different card or account.
- Your bank or payment provider typically takes **5 to 10 business days** to show
  the money, depending on the method and the country. If it has not arrived after
  that, contact us and we will chase it with the provider.

## Your statutory rights

Nothing in this policy reduces rights you have under the law of your country of
residence, and where those rights are more generous than this policy, they
prevail.

**Consumers in the EU, the UK, and Switzerland:** you have a 14-day right of
withdrawal from the moment of purchase. Because our product is digital content
delivered immediately, by completing a purchase you ask us to begin delivering it
right away, and you acknowledge that once credits have been spent on generated
content, you lose the right to withdraw with respect to what has already been
supplied. Unspent credits remain refundable within the 14 days described above —
which is at least as generous as the statutory minimum.

## Chargebacks

If something is wrong, please email us before contacting your bank. A chargeback
takes weeks to resolve and usually ends with the same refund; an email usually
ends with it the same day. We do not close accounts for asking us for a refund.
