## Credits

### How do credits work?

One credit buys one illustrated page. The cover illustration is included at no
extra charge. A book is 4 to 15 pages, so it costs between 4 and 15 credits, and
the exact number is shown before you confirm.

Writing the story first is free — credits are only spent when you confirm the
book and the illustrations are drawn.

### What do I get for free?

A new account starts with 10 free credits, which is one complete 10-page book.
No credit card is needed to try it.

### Do credits expire?

It depends how you got them:

- **Bought in a credit pack** — they never expire. They stay in your account
  until you use them.
- **Granted by a subscription** — they expire at the end of the month they were
  granted for, and do not carry over. Cancel and you keep the credits for the
  period you have already paid for.
- **The free signup credits** — these have an expiry date; it is shown in
  Settings, then Credits.

### Which credits are used first?

Whichever ones expire soonest, so a subscription's credits are spent before the
permanent ones you bought. You can see the balance and the full history in
Settings, then Credits.

## Billing and cancelling

### How do I cancel my subscription?

You can do it yourself, in four clicks, without contacting anyone:

1. Sign in.
2. Go to **Settings**, then **Billing**.
3. Press **Cancel** next to your subscription.
4. Confirm.

Cancellation takes effect at the end of the period you have already paid for.
Until then you keep full access and the credits for that period. There is no
cancellation fee and no notice period. See the
[Refund & Cancellation Policy](/refund-policy) for the detail.

### Where are my invoices and receipts?

Settings, then Billing — the payments table lists every charge with its invoice
link.

### What happens to my credits if I cancel?

The credits from the current period stay usable until that period ends. Credits
you bought in a pack are yours and never expire, so they are unaffected.

## Refunds

### Can I get a refund?

Unused credits are refundable within 14 days of purchase; credits already spent
on illustrations are not, because the computing behind them has already been
consumed. If a book failed because of a problem on our side, the credits for it
are returned automatically — you do not need to ask.

The full rules, including how to request a refund and how long it takes, are in
the [Refund & Cancellation Policy](/refund-policy).

## Your photos and your child's privacy

### What happens to the photos I upload?

They are used to draw the illustrations in that book, and nothing else:

1. The photo is stored in our own storage.
2. When a book is confirmed, we make a private copy for that book and send that
   copy to the AI provider that draws the illustrations.
3. When the book finishes, both the copy **and the original upload you sent us**
   are deleted from our storage.
4. If a generation fails, the photos are kept so you can retry without uploading
   them again — they are deleted when you delete the book.

See the [Privacy Policy](/privacy-policy) for the full picture, including what
our AI providers do with the copy while a book is being drawn.

### Can I use photos of my child?

Yes — that is what the feature is for. You must be the child's parent or legal
guardian, or have their permission, and the same goes for any other person in a
photo you upload. Do not upload photos of other people's children.

### Do the illustrations really look like the child?

They are drawn to match the photos, and results vary with the photo: a clear,
well-lit picture of one person works much better than a group shot or a blurry
one. Illustrations cannot be redrawn after a book is generated, so check the
photos before you confirm.

## Your account

### How do I delete my account?

Go to **Settings**, then **Profile**, and press **Delete my account**. You will
be asked to type the email address on the account to confirm.

If you have an active subscription, cancel it first — deleting an account does
not stop a subscription from renewing, so we ask you to cancel from Billing
instead.

Deleting your account removes your storybooks, illustrations, uploaded photos,
remaining credits and your sign-in. The only thing we keep is your billing
records, which tax and accounting rules require us to retain. This cannot be
undone.

### Can I download my books first?

Yes, and you should if you want to keep them. Every finished book has a
**Download PDF** button in your library, and once your account is deleted the
books are gone.

## Keeping stories safe

### How do you keep the stories safe for children?

Every idea is checked before a story is written. Violence, sexual content,
self-harm, hateful language, drugs, alcohol and gambling are refused, in English
and in Chinese — and the same check runs on the names you type, because those get
printed in the book.

The check runs again on the story itself: on what the model writes, and on any
page you rewrite yourself in the review step. Nothing is illustrated until the
whole story has passed. **If a story is refused, you are not charged for it** —
a draft that fails costs nothing, and a book that fails has its credits returned
automatically.

Behind the check, the prompts that write the stories carry their own child-safety
instructions: the book is read aloud to a child aged 2 to 10, often at bedtime, so
a model asked for something frightening is told to keep the feeling and leave the
harm out.

None of that is a guarantee, and we would rather hear about it than not. If a book
does not feel right for a child, tell us its name and we will look at it.

## Troubleshooting

### A book has been stuck on "Creating…" for a while

Illustrations are drawn in parallel, and a provider occasionally stalls. A book
that has not finished within 15 minutes is treated as failed, and the credits
are returned to your account automatically. You can start it again from your
idea, and you still have the story — the draft is free to regenerate.

### The story used a built-in template instead of a text model

That message means no text model is available, so the story came from our
fallback template. It is worth telling us: the fix is on the admin side, under
Settings then AI.

### A page's illustration looks wrong

Generative models make mistakes, and the occasional odd hand or strange animal
is one of them. Tell us which book and which page — we will look at whether it
can be regenerated. (Regenerating costs credits like any generation, unless the
problem is on our side.)
