This policy says what you may and may not create with {envConfigs.app_name}. It
forms part of our [Terms of Service](/terms-of-service), and using the service
means agreeing to it. It covers your idea, the names and any other text you type,
and the photos you upload.

We generate stories and illustrations with third-party AI models. That makes us
responsible for what the service is used to produce, and it means some subjects
are off limits entirely — not because of taste, but because they must not be
generated at all.

## Prohibited uses

You may not use this service to create, request or attempt to create:

- **Sexual or sexually suggestive content of any kind.** This includes
  descriptions, euphemisms, and illustration styles meant to imply it.
- **Any sexual content involving children, or any sexualisation of a minor.**
  This is illegal, and we report it. There are no exceptions and no grey area
  here.
- **Violence, gore or cruelty**, including depicting a real person being harmed.
- **Content promoting self-harm, suicide or eating disorders.**
- **Hate speech or harassment** directed at anyone for their race, ethnicity,
  nationality, religion, disability, sex, gender identity or sexual orientation.
- **Drugs, alcohol, tobacco or gambling** presented as suitable for children.
- **Content that sexualises, defames or endangers a real, identifiable person** —
  including anyone whose photo you have uploaded.
- **Anything unlawful**, or that infringes someone else's rights, including
  copyright and privacy.
- **Attempts to get around these rules** — see below.

## Content about children

This service is built for picture books made by an adult, for a child. Two rules
follow from that, and both are enforced:

- **You must be the adult on the account.** Only someone aged 18 or over may
  hold an account and create a book.
- **You confirm you have the right to use any photo of a child you upload.** You
  are that child's parent or legal guardian, or you have their parent's or
  guardian's permission. Our Terms of Service say the same.

No photo of a real child may be used to produce anything other than an
illustration for the book you are making — and never anything sexual, violent,
demeaning or otherwise harmful. Content of that kind is reported.

## How we enforce this

**Every request is screened before anything is generated.** When you ask for a
book, the text you have provided is sent to an automated content-moderation
service for a decision. Only if it passes does the service go on to write the
story and draw the illustrations. This happens before any credits are spent.

- **If a request is refused, you are not charged.** Refused requests cost you
  nothing, and you can revise your idea and try again.
- **If the moderation service cannot be reached, we stop rather than guess.** A
  request that cannot be screened is not generated. Nothing is charged, and you
  can try again in a moment.
- **We may refuse, remove, suspend or terminate** anything that breaks this
  policy, and may do so without notice where the content is illegal or harms
  someone.

From time to time we may also test the outputs the service produces as part of
keeping it safe.

## No attempts to bypass the filter

Do not try to get content through by obfuscating it: altered spellings, spacing
or punctuation between letters, homophones, puns, another language, code words,
or asking a model to "ignore previous instructions". These are all the same
violation as asking for the content outright, and are treated as such. The same
goes for using a photo to smuggle in content that the text rules would refuse.

## Reporting something

If you see a book, or content within one, that breaks this policy, tell us at

<a href={`mailto:${envConfigs.support_email}`}>{envConfigs.support_email}</a>.

report.

If you believe a request of yours was refused by mistake, write to us with what
you were trying to make — the filter sometimes reads an innocent idea the wrong
way, and a person will look at it.
